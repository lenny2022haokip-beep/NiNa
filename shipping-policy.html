Policies

Shipping Policy

Thank you for shopping with us. Please review our shipping policy below to understand how we process and deliver your orders.

1. Processing Time

All orders are processed within 3–4 business days after being placed.

Please note that orders are not dispatched on Sundays or Government Holidays.

2. Delivery Time

Delivery timelines vary depending on your location:

  • Kangpokpi District Area: 1–2 business days
  • North East (NE) Circle: 4–5 business days
  • Other Parts of India: 7–8 business days

Please note that delivery times are estimates and may vary due to unforeseen circumstances. For any delays, an update message and mail will be sent to you via WhatsApp and Email.

3. Shipping Charges

  • Kangpokpi District Area: Free shipping
  • Rest of India: Shipping charges are calculated based on the weight and number of products purchased

4. Tracking Your Order

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via WhatsApp and email.

If you do not receive your tracking details, please feel free to contact us for assistance.

5. International Shipping & Customs

  • International shipping is currently unavailable
  • Therefore, customs and international duties do not apply at this time

We use India Post (Speed Post) as our trusted delivery partner to ensure safe and reliable shipping.

Returns & Exchanges

Since each of our traditional Puans, bags, scarves, and accessories is individually handloomed, we maintain a high standard of quality and craftsmanship.

Policy

We do not accept returns or exchanges unless the product is received in a damaged condition due to shipping.

Process

To report a damaged item, customers must provide clear video and image proof of the product and contact us via WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery. After verification, appropriate action will be taken.

Support

If you have any questions regarding our shipping policy or need assistance with an order, please reach out to us on WhatsApp.
